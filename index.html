Set among verdant gardens and quiet lanes, Green Villa offers four independent villas each designed for comfort and privacy. Our team curates local experiences — from hands-on cooking classes to immersive cultural and nature tours.

This homestay was a lovely experience. I appreciated the quieter location outside the city (around half and hour away from Lover's Leap waterfall), and as someone using public transport on my own, it was easily accessible. My room was comfortable and clean with a nice garden view, and the blankets were nice and cozy. The shared bathroom was always clean, which was good. It was also a very affordable and safe place to stay. I ordered breakfast and dinner, and the Sri Lankan dinner was so good – you could taste the freshness of the vegetables, from around Nuwara Eliya, likely some from their own garden. The hosts were genuinely kind and made everyone feel welcome. It's a real homestay, so being respectful of their house rules, like curfew is part of the experience.
