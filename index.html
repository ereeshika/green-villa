About Green Villa

Nestled in the heart of Nuwara Eliya, Green Villa offers an exceptional hospitality experience combining comfort, luxury, and authentic Sri Lankan charm.

Our Story

Green Villa has been welcoming guests from around the world with warm Sri Lankan hospitality. Our commitment to excellence and attention to detail ensures every guest experiences the best of what Nuwara Eliya has to offer.

With four distinct villas, we cater to groups of all sizes, from intimate gatherings to large family reunions and corporate retreats. Each villa is thoughtfully designed with modern amenities while preserving the natural beauty of our surroundings.

Why Choose Us?

  • Prime location in Nuwara Eliya
  • Four unique villas with different capacities
  • Modern amenities and traditional charm
  • Local cooking classes and guided tours
  • Exceptional customer service
  • Easy booking through multiple platforms
